STATION LINKS

For classroom stations: no codes, every built building open. Paste a link into Google Classroom or on the board. Nothing is saved to your sheet, and nothing counts toward any student's real progress.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Stars earned at a station last only until the tab is closed, so the next student at that Chromebook starts fresh.
  • A town link keeps students in that town: the "world map" sign is hidden. EXIT starts the town again.
  • A station tab shows a STATION: ALL OPEN banner, so you can tell which mode a Chromebook is in.
  • To go back to normal sign-in in the same tab, add ?station=off to the address (or just close the tab).
  • These links are hidden, not locked: anyone who has one can open every building.
