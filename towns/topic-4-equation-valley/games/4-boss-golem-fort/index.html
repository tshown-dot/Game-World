MISSION BRIEFING: GOLEM FORT
Equation Valley · Topic 4 Boss · Lessons 4-1 to 4-10
1. BREAK THE GOLEM

The Equation Golem has five parts: feet, arms, shoulders, shield and core. Break them one at a time.

2. CHOOSE YOUR WEAPON

Each part is broken with a machine from the valley. Pick one, then play its hardest zone. Clear the zone and the part shatters.

3. STAKES

If a weapon breaks (you lose all 3 lives inside it), the golem takes one of your 3 hearts. Lose them all and you start that part again. Only a timed run defeats the golem.
