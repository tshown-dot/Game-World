1. YOU HOLD A KEY

Your key is a number. Every lock is an equation. The key opens a lock only if it makes the equation true.

2. TAP WHAT IT OPENS

Locks roll past. Put your key in for the letter and check both sides. Tap every lock it opens and let the rest roll by. Later you choose the keys, and forge a lock of your own.

3. STAKES

A jammed lock, or an open one you let go, raises the ALARM. Fill it and a guard comes. Lose all 3 chances and you're caught. Beat the Locksmith to win!
