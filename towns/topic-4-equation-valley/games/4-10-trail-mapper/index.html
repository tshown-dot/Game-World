MISSION BRIEFING: TRAIL MAPPER
Equation Valley · Lesson 4-10 · Relate Tables, Graphs, and Equations
1. ONE TRAIL, THREE WAYS

Every trail has a rule (an equation), a logbook (a table) and a map (a graph). They all say the same thing.

d = 3t : (2, 6) : 2 across, 6 up

2. SET IT, THEN SEND THE SCOUT

Put stakes on the map, slide a pointer along an axis, or set the rule on the dials. Then the scout walks the real trail and shows what matches.

3. STAKES

Every mistake makes the scout more TIRED. When it fills, a canteen is gone. Lose all 3 canteens and the trip is over. Cross Rockslide Pass to win!
