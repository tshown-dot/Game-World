1. THE GEAR AND THE FLAG

A gear rolls along the toothed track. Every turn moves it the same number of teeth, and it has to stop exactly on the flag.

4x = 36 : 4 turns of x teeth

2. SET IT, THEN ROLL

The letter is the part you set: the teeth per turn, the number of turns, or where the flag goes. Then roll the gear and watch it count.

3. STAKES

Stop short or roll past, and the STRAIN rises. When it fills, a spare gear breaks. Lose all 3 spare gears and the gearbox jams. Free the Stuck Gearbox to win!
