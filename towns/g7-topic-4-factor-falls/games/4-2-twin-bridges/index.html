Factor Falls · Grade 7 Lesson 4-2 · Generate Equivalent Expressions
1. BRIDGES AND POSTS

Each expression is a bridge. Each post is a value of the letter, and the bridge's plank sits at the height the expression gives there.

2. TWINS MATCH EVERYWHERE

Two expressions are equivalent when their bridges match at every post, not just one. A hiker crossing on both falls at the first post where they split.

3. STAKES

Every mistake raises the FLOOD. When it fills, a rope snaps. Lose all 3 ropes and the bridges wash out. Make the Flood Crossing to win!
