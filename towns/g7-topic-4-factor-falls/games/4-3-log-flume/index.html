MISSION BRIEFING: LOG FLUME
Factor Falls · Grade 7 Lesson 4-3 · Simplify Expressions
1. LOGS ARE TERMS

Every log carries one term of an expression, with its sign. Like terms belong in the same channel.

−2c + 3c − 5 − 4c + 7

2. SORT, THEN ADD

Drag each log to its channel (or press its number). Then add up each channel to get the simplest form.

−3c + 2

3. STAKES

The flume BACKS UP while you think, and a log in the wrong channel jams it. Lose all 3 poles and the flume is blocked. Clear the Log Jam to win!
