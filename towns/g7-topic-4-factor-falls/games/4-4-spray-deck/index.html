1. A DECK IS A PRODUCT

The number outside the parentheses is the deck's width. Each term inside is the length of one section.

5(x + 8)

2. EXPAND IT

Type the area of every section: width × length. The width multiplies each term, signs included. Some decks then need simplifying.

5x + 40
